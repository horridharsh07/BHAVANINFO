LEGAL & STATUTORY REPOSITORY

Department Rules, Regulations & Statutory Directives

All cadastral registrations, building mutations, and drone audits in the State of Punjab are strictly governed by the following statutory bye-laws and regulations.

STATUTORY ENFORCEMENT

Section 187, Punjab Municipal Act (1911 & 2026 AI Amendments)

Notification Ref: PB-MCA-SEC187-2026-REV • Statutory Authority: ADC (Revenue)

3D Vertical Height & FAR Compliance: Any structural addition beyond the sanctioned number of storeys or approved Floor Area Ratio (FAR) detected via autonomous LiDAR drone SLAM survey triggers an automated Statutory 24-Hour Notice.

  • The owner must submit a regularisation petition or explain the discrepancy within 48 hours.
  • Failure to report results in automatic cancellation of provisional registration and municipal demolition notice under Sec 187.
  • Challan fees deposited via Bharatkosh are forfeited as municipal statutory penalty.
CADASTRAL NUMBERING

Bhu-Aadhaar 14-Digit ULPIN & 3D Sub-ULPIN Standard Operating Procedure

Ministry of Rural Development Directive • Standard: DoLR-SOP-2024-ULPIN

Unique Land Parcel Identification Number (ULPIN): Every parcel is assigned a geo-referenced 14-digit alphanumeric Bhu-Aadhaar code derived from longitude and latitude centroids.

  • Vertical Levels (Sub-ULPIN): Multi-storey structures assign sub-units (e.g. BCN501B1NA2CH0-G00 for Ground Floor, F01 for Floor 1).
  • Foundation Tier: Subterranean layers receive dedicated Sub-ULPIN codes (e.g. B30-UTL) to demarcate public right-of-way.
  • No property transaction or bank mortgage can be registered without verified 14-digit ULPIN accreditation.
DRONE OPERATIONS

DGCA Green-Zone Autonomous Cadastral LiDAR SLAM Guidelines

DGCA Drone Rules 2021/2026 • Precision Tier: 3R Cadastral ($le 2.5 ext{ cm}$)

Autonomous Cadastral Flight Corridors: Survey drones operate within authorized green-zones using Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) differential GPS and triple-return LiDAR scanners.

  • Drone missions occur between 07:00 AM and 05:00 PM on working days under municipal flight authorization.
  • Raw point clouds are processed in high-density voxel grids with automated building footprint snapping.
  • Citizens receive advance SMS telemetry alerts prior to autonomous flight execution over their khasra.
SUBTERRANEAN SAFETY

Subterranean Utility Corridor & Right-of-Way (RoW) Safe Digging Bye-Laws

Municipal Corporation of Amritsar (MCA) By-Law 44 • Gas & Utility Protection

Mandatory Utility Buffer: A strict 5.0-meter horizontal and 3.0-meter vertical buffer must be preserved around high-pressure gas mains (PNG), municipal water trunk conduits, and high-voltage power feeders.

  • Basement excavation encroaching into municipal gas conduits is an immediate non-compoundable offense.
  • Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) validation is legally compulsory before foundation pile driving.
  • Telemetry feeds into the Unified Subterranean Asset Registry (USAR).
FINANCIAL PROTOCOL

Bharatkosh Non-Tax Receipt Portal (NTRP) & Fee Schedule

Account Head: 0029 - Land Revenue • Survey SLA: Next 2 Working Days

PUBLIC TRANSPARENCY & SERVICES

Citizen Charter & Frequently Asked Questions

Under the Right to Information Act (RTI 2005) and the Punjab Transparency in Public Services Act, citizens have the right to transparent, timely delivery of revenue services.

❓ How do I verify my building's 3D Digital Twin?

Click on the Citizen / Officer Sign In button on the top navigation bar. Authenticate using your Aadhaar linked mobile number or JanParichay ID. Your properties will be automatically indexed under your Bhu-Aadhaar portfolio where you can launch the 3D Inspector.

❓ What happens if my building receives a 24-hour statutory notice?

Under Section 187 of the Punjab Municipal Act, an automated LiDAR anomaly notice is issued if an unauthorized vertical extension or height violation is detected. You can view the exact 3D LiDAR discrepancy in the portal and submit an electronic appeal or regularization challan before statutory expiry.

❓ When will the autonomous drone LiDAR survey be conducted?

Upon submitting a new property registration and paying the e-challan via Bharatkosh Gateway, an autonomous drone survey mission is automatically scheduled for dispatch within the next two working days.

❓ How does the portal protect against subterranean utility accidents?

BHAVANINFO integrates Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) layers down to -30ft depth, visualizing city gas pipelines, electrical conduits, potable water lines, and sanitary sewer culverts. Safe digging zones are displayed in real-time.
